Laid out in the nineteenth century as a terrace over the city, with a bronze copy of the David in the middle and souvenir stalls along the edge. From about an hour before sunset the parapet is three people deep and coaches queue on the road behind. Everything the photographs promise is genuinely there. So is the crowd. Giardino delle Rose is one terrace below and San Miniato is eight minutes above, both free and both quieter.
