Molo means jetty, and that is roughly the position: down on the riverside rather than up on the lungarno. Works as a daytime stop and as an evening one, with the river carrying most of the atmosphere. Seasonal in practice, since the appeal collapses in the rain. Confirm the season and hours before going.
