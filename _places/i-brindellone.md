Plain cream walls, no decoration to speak of, and a room small enough that turning up without a reservation usually means being turned away. Tuscan cooking without adjustment. The steak ritual is the thing people remember: the cut arrives at the table first, you agree to it, then it goes to the grill. Further from the centre than the trattorias people queue for, which is most of why it stays like this.
