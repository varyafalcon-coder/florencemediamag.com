The Odeon opened as a cinema in 1922 inside Palazzo dello Strozzino, under a domed ceiling of coloured glass with gilded stucco. It ran for close to a century, then reopened in November 2023 as a bookshop and cinema together.

Around twenty five thousand titles including originals in English, a cafe with a wine window, and the old hall still screening films in their original language with Italian subtitles. During the day the upper gallery is open with seating and nobody hurries you out.

Piazza degli Strozzi, five minutes on foot from Piazza della Repubblica.
