Tullio Fontana opened this in 1896 as a Florentine answer to the literary cafes of Paris: somewhere to meet, argue and stay a while. It is still on Viale Galileo, halfway up the Viale dei Colli, surrounded by trees and olives rather than streets.

Today it works as several things at once. A cafe and bistrot for a coffee or an aperitivo, and a restaurant with a menu that follows the seasons, partly out of its own orto and nursery on the property.

Bus 12 from Porta Romana, or on foot as part of the Viale dei Colli walk. A short way below Piazzale Michelangelo and far quieter than it.
