Opened in 2022 two minutes from Piazza della Signoria, which puts it directly on the route most families are already walking. The Pick a Brick wall lets children fill a cup with loose bricks by hand, and the Mosaic Maker photographs you and prints the build instructions for a portrait in bricks.

It is a shop and it is priced like one. Treated as twenty minutes of something tactile between two things that require standing still, it earns its place.
