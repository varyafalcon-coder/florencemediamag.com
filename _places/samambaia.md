Run by Pepe, who rotates the menu monthly through a different cuisine and cooks it properly rather than as a theme. Alongside the rotation there are the regulars people come back for: eggs, avocado toast, hash browns, cakes, and a matcha latte that gets its own reviews.

The room is warm, informal and unusually welcoming, which is the reason visitors tend to return several times in one trip rather than once. Gluten free options are marked on the menu, and dogs get water and a treat.

Opens at nine, which makes it one of the few places in Florence serving a real breakfast before the bars fill up. Via Montebello, about ten minutes on foot west of Santa Maria Novella station.
