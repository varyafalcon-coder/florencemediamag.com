Opened in 2019 on Lungarno Pecori Giraldi, east of the tourist centre. Salsa and bachata most nights, live music, mojitos, Cuban sandwiches and pizza.

The afternoon is the part worth knowing about. Loungers face the Arno and nobody minds if you sit there with a drink and do nothing. Bus 14 from Santa Maria Novella station, or twenty five minutes on foot east along the river from Ponte alle Grazie.
