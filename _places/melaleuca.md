Everything is made on site: sourdough, bagels, cured salmon, smoked bacon, the cakes. The coffee is roasted locally. No bookings, so at weekends there is a queue and a wait, and the riverside tables go first. The crowd is students, expats and Florentines who want a break from a standing espresso and a cornetto. Closes mid-afternoon.
