The garden climbs the hill behind Ponte alle Grazie with a baroque staircase up the middle and a belvedere at the top. The wisteria tunnel flowers from about mid April to early May and is the one moment it gets busy. It stays quiet the rest of the year because Boboli sits next door and absorbs the entire crowd. Ticketed. There is a cafe on the terrace if you want to sit with the view.
