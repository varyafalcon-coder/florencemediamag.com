Most visitors reach the Piazzale, photograph the skyline and turn around, so the crowd thins almost completely on the last stretch of the climb. Green and white marble facade, a crypt, and a cemetery behind the church worth an extra fifteen minutes. The monks sing Gregorian vespers in the late afternoon. The time moves with the season and published sources disagree on it, so read the notice at the door.
