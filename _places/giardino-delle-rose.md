It sits on the walk up from San Niccolo, just below the Piazzale, and almost everyone walks past the gate without noticing it. The view is framed by greenery instead of souvenir stalls, which changes the photograph and the experience. Roses peak in May, but the terraces work year round and there is grass to sit on.
