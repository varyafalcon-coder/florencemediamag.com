The proof that being next to the most crowded bridge in Italy does not have to mean what it usually means. A dozen seats at the counter, a few tables outside on Piazza dei Rossi, and a list that changes constantly because they buy from small estates rather than distributors. Ask what they opened today. Cheese and cured meats to go with it.
