The sign outside says nothing and the front looks like every other family trattoria in the city. Inside, the walls and ceiling are covered floor to ceiling in scrawl left by customers, the service is loud and deliberately teasing, and the kitchen sends out onion soup, wild boar pasta and a proper fiorentina.

The house calls its own style goliardico, which is roughly student humour with a straight face. If you want a quiet dinner this is the wrong room. If you want the version of a Florentine trattoria that locals actually book, it is the right one.

Ten minutes on foot north of the Duomo up Via San Gallo, towards Piazza della Liberta.
