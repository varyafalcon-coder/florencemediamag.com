The old convent complex now holds craft workshops and artists' studios, and the cafe sits in the cloister at the middle of them. There is a newspaper and book corner, work desks with free wifi, pinsa and salads at prices that have not been adjusted for visitors, and a green courtyard that is open to the public.

The courtyard is the reason to come. Children can run around it safely, you can sit with a laptop for hours without anyone hurrying you, and the artisan workshops are reachable straight from it. Cultural events run through the year, more of them in summer.

Open Tuesday to Saturday 9:00 to 23:00 and Sunday 9:00 to 20:00. Via Giano della Bella, about fifteen minutes on foot southwest of Ponte Vecchio.
