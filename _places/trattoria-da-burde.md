It started on Via di Peretola and moved to Via Pistoiese in 1927. Cooking of the outer suburbs rather than the centre: ribollita, pappa al pomodoro, bistecca, fresh pasta made daily. You need a bus or a car to get here, and that filter is the whole point. One of the few places in the city where the room would look the same if no visitors existed at all.
