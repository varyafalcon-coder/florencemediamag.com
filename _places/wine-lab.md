Vino sfuso is how Florentines bought wine before it came with a label and a price to match, and the practice never fully disappeared in this part of the city. Via Gioberti is a working shopping street with no monuments on it, which is why almost no visitor ends up here. Open through the day until ten, closed Sunday.
