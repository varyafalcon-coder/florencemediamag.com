Robert Marrandi worked out of pop-up locations for three years before opening here permanently in September 2022. One room, ovens in view, the smell reaching the corner of Via di Camaldoli. Cinnamon and cardamom are the constants, everything else rotates with the season: salted caramel, rhubarb, apple crumble, lemon poppyseed. There is sourdough and a savoury side too, brunch buns and sandwiches. Small, so it fills up.
