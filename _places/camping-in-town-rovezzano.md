The odd one on this list and the least Florentine, which is exactly why it is quiet when the municipal pools are full. Out at Rovezzano, past Bellariva, with a pool, grass and the general holiday atmosphere of a campsite. Worth knowing about in the middle of August, when half the city is queueing for the other four.
