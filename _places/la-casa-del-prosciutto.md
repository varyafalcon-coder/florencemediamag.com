Mugello tortelli are potato filled and thicker than elsewhere, and this is one of the addresses Florentines drive out for. Wild boar or duck ragu on top, fried mushrooms alongside. Around forty minutes from the city, through hills that look nothing like the postcard Tuscany further south.
