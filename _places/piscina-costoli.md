An olympic tank, a diving pool and one for small children. Campo di Marte is a sports district and the pool has the same character: serious, well run, no decoration. The place to go if you actually want to swim lengths rather than sit. Caps compulsory, under fourteens need an adult, and identity documents are checked at the entrance. Summer season runs roughly from the second week of June to the end of August.
