The name is literal. Wine on shelves floor to ceiling, a marble counter, no tables to speak of, and a plate of crostini or a panino to go with the glass. It works on the market's rhythm rather than the tourist one, which means it is busiest in the middle of the day and shut when the market is. The kind of place where standing next to strangers is the format, not an inconvenience.
