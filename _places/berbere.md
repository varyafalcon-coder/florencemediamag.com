The dough is naturally leavened and fermented long, which is the reason people describe it as light rather than filling. Toppings change with the season and are usually added off the heat, slice by slice. It arrives already cut, which is a deliberate push toward ordering two between two people and swapping. Three addresses in the city, the one on Piazza de' Nerli being the neighbourhood option.
