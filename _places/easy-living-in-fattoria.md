Same idea as the beach under San Niccolo, moved out of the city: deck chairs, a bar, food, music in the evening, and open air instead of stone. Worth going for the change of scale rather than for anything specific on the menu. Seasonal, and the programme changes through the summer, so check what is on before setting out.
