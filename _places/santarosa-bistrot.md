Open since 2016 on Lungarno di Santa Rosa, at the far end of San Frediano near the gate. The garden does most of the work: benches, trees, chairs on the grass, lights in the evening. The kitchen changes its menu roughly every three months with the seasons. It runs from morning until midnight, but aperitivo is what it is really for, and by eight the lawn is full.
