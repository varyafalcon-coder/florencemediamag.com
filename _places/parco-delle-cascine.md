Big enough to lose the city in, which is not true of anywhere else in Florence. Avenues of plane trees, grass, playgrounds, the pool at Le Pavoniere, and a large market on Tuesday mornings. Nobody comes here on a city break, so on a weekday it is Florentines walking dogs and students running. Reachable by tram.
