The rule is the concept: everything poured here is made in Italy, which turns a familiar cocktail list into something unfamiliar. Negronis, vermouth, amari and a lot of things you will not have tried.

Small, dark, and serious about the drinks without being solemn. Five minutes west of Piazza della Repubblica.
