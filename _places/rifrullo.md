The place has been at this address since 1917 and describes itself as the first American bar in Florence. It now works as three things depending on the hour: coffee in the morning, lunch and brunch in the middle, cocktails and DJ sets later in the week. The garden is enclosed and shaded, and the terrace looks at the city walls just below Piazzale Michelangelo. Five minutes from Ponte alle Grazie and still off the walking route.
