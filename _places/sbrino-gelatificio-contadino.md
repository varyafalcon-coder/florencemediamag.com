Around eighteen cream flavours and eight sorbets, changing with what is available. Vegan and gluten free options are properly made rather than an afterthought. This is the address for people who want to know where the milk came from, and it is a different proposition from the historic counters in the centre. Also has a second shop on Via Senese.
