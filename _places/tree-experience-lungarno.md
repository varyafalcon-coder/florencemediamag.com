The first adventure park to open in central Florence rather than out in the hills. Courses are graded by height rather than age, so small children get the suspended net rooms and the bouncing pillows while older ones and adults take the rope bridges and ziplines. There is minigolf as well.

Green, shaded and directly on the river, which makes it the rare thing in this city: somewhere a child can be loud for two hours without anybody minding. Habana 500, with loungers facing the water, is a few minutes along the same stretch.
