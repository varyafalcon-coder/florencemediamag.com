Three rooms, a hundred covers, tables close together and a noise level to match. The oil and the house wine come from the family farm. Fresh pasta is made in house and the menu moves with the season, but the curiosities stay: tortellini from a Bolognese recipe carried in by the owner's father, and prawns with curry and mango chutney, on the menu because British customers asked for something familiar seventy years ago. Prices are well above a neighbourhood trattoria and reviews argue about whether that is fair. Closed Tuesday, and Wednesday and Thursday at lunch.
