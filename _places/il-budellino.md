Small, cheap and entirely local. A glass of red, a plate of cheese and cured meat, and a crowd that spills onto the pavement because there is nowhere else to stand.

This is aperitivo in its oldest form: no buffet, no cocktail list, no view. Just wine at a price that has not moved much and people you end up talking to.
