The whole idea is stated in the name: the kitchen works with a small set of producers rather than suppliers, and the menu names them. Long fermentation dough, organic flour, Tuscan oil, and a room that is bright and plain rather than rustic.

Two minutes from the Medici Chapels, which puts it in the middle of the tourist streets while behaving nothing like them.
