The Bini workshop made hats here, then design objects, and the bar kept the machinery instead of clearing it out. It works as a bar, a bookshelf and a small museum at once. Aperitivo comes with food included, which is why the tables outside go first and why the bill here is noticeably lower than at the place two rows over. Open until around two.
