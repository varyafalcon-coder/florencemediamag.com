Three pools, grass, shade, a bar and restaurant inside, and prices set by the city rather than by a hotel. In winter it belongs to the swimming clubs and in summer it opens to everyone, which makes it a genuine neighbourhood pool full of families. Reachable by bus from the station. Open from early June to about mid September.
