The business goes back further than the family: the room was a working-class canteen in the early twentieth century and a convent refectory before that. They were evicted from Borgo San Frediano in 1999 and moved a few hundred metres to Via Pisana, changing nothing. Terracotta floors, farm tools on the walls, a daily menu in Italian only. You share a table with whoever is there, which on a given day means a builder and a banker. Arrive at noon or queue.
