The zoological wing of the university's natural history museum, and one of the oldest science museums in Europe open to the public. The hippopotamus was a gift to the Grand Duke, lived in Boboli, and was reassembled after its death by craftsmen who had only read a description of one.

The eighteenth century wax anatomy collection sits at the far end. Signposted, and easy to walk past if it is not for you.

Ten minutes on foot from Ponte Vecchio, up Via Guicciardini and left along Via Romana.
