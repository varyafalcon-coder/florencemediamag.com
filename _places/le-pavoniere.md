The closest pool to the historic centre and the one Florentines name first. There is a children's pool as well as the main tank, loungers to rent, and a restaurant that stays open through the day. In the evening the water closes and the grounds turn into a garden club with music. Swimming caps are compulsory, which surprises visitors every year. Open roughly June to mid September.
