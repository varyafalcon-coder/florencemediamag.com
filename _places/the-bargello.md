Two Davids by Donatello, the competition panels that started the Renaissance argument over the Baptistery doors, and a courtyard covered in coats of arms. Sculpture asks less of you than painting and rewards a shorter visit, which makes this the right first museum rather than the fourth. Ten minutes from the Duomo and almost never full.
