A quiet stretch of river through the middle of the city, which sounds unlikely until you are on it. Suitable for beginners and for families, with no whitewater involved. The stretch runs through the historic centre, so the whole thing is a view of Florence that almost nobody has. Seasonal, roughly March to November.
