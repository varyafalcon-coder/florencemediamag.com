The building opened in 1896 as Florence's first shop selling household goods, and the current owners kept the name and the industrial bones when they reopened it. It now runs as several rooms at once: a florist near the entrance, a bistro, a full restaurant, and a bar downstairs with live music.

It is open from breakfast until late, which is rare here, so it works equally as a morning coffee with a laptop and as somewhere to end the evening. Busy at weekend brunch.

Via de' Ginori, five minutes north of the Duomo.
