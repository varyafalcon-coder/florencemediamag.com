The complex was a prison until 1985 and is now housing, studios and a public courtyard. The cafe runs from morning coffee through to late drinks, with concerts, readings and film nights through the year.

Central but almost entirely local, because visitors do not think to walk into a former prison. Ten minutes east of the Duomo.
