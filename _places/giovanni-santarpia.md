Before Santarpia the raised cornicione was rare here and fried pizza was rarer. He came from Castellammare di Stabia, opened first in Chianti and pulled Florentines out of the city to eat pizza, then moved into town. The menu keeps the Naples classics and adds local jokes, including a fried pizzetta filled with lampredotto. Always full, so book. Sources disagree on who is at the oven now, so ask if it matters to you.
