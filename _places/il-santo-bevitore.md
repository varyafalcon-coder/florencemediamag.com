The name comes from a Joseph Roth novella and the tone matches: unfussy room, candlelight, no decoration doing any work. Cooking is Tuscan with a lighter hand than a trattoria, and the wine list runs deep in Tuscany without stopping there. Book, because it fills, and its smaller wine bar sits a few doors down the same street.
