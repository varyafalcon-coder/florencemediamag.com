Frederick Stibbert was born in Florence to a British family, inherited a fortune and spent it on about fifty thousand objects: European, Islamic and Japanese arms and armour, costume, furniture, tapestries. He left it all to the city when he died in 1906, and it is still arranged the way he left it.

The Sala della Cavalcata is the room people remember. Life sized figures in real armour, European and Ottoman, mounted and drawn up in formation down one long hall.

The park outside is free and usually empty: an English style garden by Giuseppe Poggi with a lake, a small Egyptian temple and grottoes. Bus 4 from Santa Maria Novella station, about fifteen minutes.
