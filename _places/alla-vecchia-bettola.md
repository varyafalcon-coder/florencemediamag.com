Open since 1979, started by the people behind Nerbone in the Mercato Centrale. You sit on wooden benches at long tables, share with strangers, and pour your own Chianti from a straw flask. Penne alla bettola, in a tomato and cream sauce with heat in it, is what most tables order. Also good for bistecca. Service is fast because they turn the room several times. Book about a week ahead. Closed Sunday and Monday.
