Simone Bonini started in Piazza Oberdan and is credited by other gelato makers with pulling the city's standards up: fresh seasonal fruit, whole milk, good chocolate, no semi-finished bases. The pozzetti counter he revived is now what you look for anywhere to tell real gelato from decoration.
