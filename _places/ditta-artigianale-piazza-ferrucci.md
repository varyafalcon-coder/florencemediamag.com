Ditta Artigianale opened in 2013 and is largely responsible for the fact that you can now get a decent flat white in this city. The Lungarno branch is the roomiest of them, with outdoor tables and a view of the water.

Good coffee, cocktails later, and food that goes well beyond a cornetto. Busy at weekend brunch, calm on a weekday morning.
