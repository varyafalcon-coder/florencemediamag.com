An unlikely thing to find above a fashion boutique on Via Roma, thirty seconds from the Duomo. Alocasia and cycas, low seating, quiet, and a menu built around cold-pressed juices, smoothies and vegetable-heavy plates with vegan and gluten free options. It works as a place to sit down for an hour in the middle of the centre when everything at street level is full. Daytime only, closes in the early evening.
