Pizza al taglio is usually the cheap option and rarely the good one. Here it is both. The dough is naturally leavened and the toppings change with the season: porchetta and chard, spianata calabra with aubergine and sheep's ricotta. You point at what you want, they weigh it, you eat it on your feet. Consistently named in city rankings for slices, which is a category most guides skip.
