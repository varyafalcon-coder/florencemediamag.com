Buca means hole, and Florence has a whole class of restaurants built into the basements of old palaces. This is one of the last obvious ones, twenty paces from the most photographed bridge in Italy and still easy to walk past. Small, cool in August because it is underground, and known for artichoke tortino, ribollita and fried artichokes in season. There is a bricked-up wine window on the wall outside, which most people walk under without looking up.
