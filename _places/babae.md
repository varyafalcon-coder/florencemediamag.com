The wine windows were cut into palace walls in the seventeenth century so wine could be sold without contact. Almost all of them were sealed. Babae reopened theirs and now runs an evening hour when you ring, order and drink standing in the street. Inside is a small dining room full of plants. The window is the reason people come, and it draws a queue, so it is more of a twenty minute stop than an evening.
