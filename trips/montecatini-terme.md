Montecatini was where Europe came to take the waters before the First World War, and the town still looks the part: avenues of plane trees, grand hotels and spa halls with marble counters where the waters were poured.

At the top of the funicular, Montecatini Alto has a small square of cafés and a view over the Valdinievole.
