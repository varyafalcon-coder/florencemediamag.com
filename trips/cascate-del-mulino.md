The water comes from the spring of the thermal resort up the road and runs down a natural channel to the waterfall by the mill, filling a staircase of pools as it goes. In winter the steam rises off the pools and the place feels empty in the early morning.

The village of Saturnia is five minutes up the hill, with a few trattorie for lunch, and the hill towns of Pitigliano, Sovana and Sorano are within half an hour.
