title: Piazza del Campo and the Torre del Mangia
seo_description: The shell-shaped square the Palio races around, and some 400 steps up the tower above
  it for the view over the roofs.
permalink: /trips/piazza-del-campo-and-the-torre-del-mangia/
facts:
- label: Tower hours
  value: About 10:00 to 16:00 in winter, to 19:00 from March to mid-October
- label: Ticket
  value: About 10 euro
go_for: The shell-shaped square, then about 400 steps up the tower for the view over the roofs of Siena.
skip_if: You are uneasy on steep, narrow stairs.
before:
- label: Tickets
  value: Sold on the day at the Palazzo Pubblico ticket office on the Campo, with a time slot. Not sold
    online.
- label: Closures
  value: Closed 25 December, and around the Palio. Heavy rain or wind can close it without notice.
