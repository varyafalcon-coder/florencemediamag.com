The square's pool was built for pilgrims on the Via Francigena, and the water steams on cold mornings. Walk to the edge of the village and down into the Parco dei Mulini, where the water once turned mill wheels cut into the rock.

The village has a few places to eat and some of the best views in the Val d'Orcia.
