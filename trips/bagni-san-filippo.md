The path starts in the hamlet of Bagni San Filippo and drops into the Fosso Bianco, the white ditch. The pools nearest the Whale are the hottest, warm enough even in January.

Combine it with the Val d'Orcia: Bagno Vignoni and Pienza are both within half an hour.
