## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">In Alta Badia lunch is planned before the runs.</p>

Wide, perfectly groomed slopes under the Sassongher and the Sella, the Sellaronda loop starting from Corvara, and quiet Ladin villages in the evening. Nowhere else in the Alps eats this well on the mountain.

## The mountain, top to bottom

<ol class="ladder">
<li><span class="alt">2,778 m</span><span class="lname">The top lifts</span><span class="ldesc">The highest point of the area, with the Sella massif in front of you.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">Slopes</span><span class="lname">Piz La Ila and Pralongià</span><span class="ldesc">Wide, sunny plateaus of easy runs and the best huts.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">Slopes</span><span class="lname">The Lagazuoi run</span><span class="ldesc">A long, quiet descent through the rock walls to Armentarola.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">1,568 m</span><span class="lname">Corvara</span><span class="ldesc">The biggest village, on the Sellaronda.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">Below</span><span class="lname">La Villa and San Cassiano</span><span class="ldesc">Smaller, quieter and the smartest evenings.</span></li>
</ol>

## A perfect day

<ol class="dayplan">
<li><span class="when">Morning</span><span class="what">Up from Corvara and round the Sellaronda while the snow is firm.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Lunch</span><span class="what">Seafood at <a href="/trips/club-moritzino/">Club Moritzino</a>, at the top of Piz La Ila.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Afternoon</span><span class="what">Easy runs across Pralongià as the light turns gold.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Next day</span><span class="what">The Lagazuoi run, and a grill at <a href="/trips/rifugio-scotoni/">Rifugio Scotoni</a> on the way down.</span></li>
</ol>

## Before you go

<ul class="tips">
<li>75 to 83&nbsp;€ for Alta Badia alone. Take Dolomiti Superski only if you will leave the valley.</li>
<li>Book lunch at the famous huts in high season, or arrive before 12:30.</li>
<li>The Sellaronda takes a whole day. Start before ten.</li>
</ul>
