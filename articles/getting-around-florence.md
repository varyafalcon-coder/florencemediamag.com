Florence is a walking city. From the station to Ponte Vecchio is fifteen minutes on foot, and from the Duomo to Santa Croce ten. Most visitors never need a ticket except to and from the airport.

<div class="callout">
<p><strong>Never drive into the centre.</strong> The ZTL is enforced by cameras, not barriers. You will not notice you have entered it until the fine arrives, sometimes months later, at home.</p>
</div>

## Tram and bus

Two tram lines connect the station with the airport (T2) and with Scandicci (T1). The airport tram takes about twenty minutes from the stop by Santa Maria Novella.

A ticket costs 2 euro and is valid for 90 minutes on trams and buses, including changes. The price went up from 1.70 on 1 August 2026; tickets bought before then stay valid until 31 January 2027. Buy it at tobacconists, newsagents, machines or in the app: bought on board from the driver, it costs 3 euro. Validate it as soon as you board.

The small electric C buses run through the centre's narrow streets and are useful with tired legs.

## Taxis

Taxis do not cruise for passengers: find them at ranks at the station and the main squares, or call one. From the airport to the centre there is a fixed fare, around 28 euro on weekdays, 30 on Sundays and holidays and 32 at night, plus a small airport supplement and a charge per bag.

## The ZTL

The *Zona a Traffico Limitato* covers the whole historic centre. Unauthorised cars cannot enter on weekdays from 7.30 to 20.00 and on Saturdays from 7.30 to 16.00, and on summer weekend nights a night ZTL applies too. Fines run from about 80 to over 300 euro.

If your hotel is inside the ZTL, it can register your plate so you can drive in to drop bags. Ask in writing before you arrive. Otherwise park outside the ring of avenues and walk or take the tram.

## Bikes

Shared bikes and scooters are available through apps, and the riverside paths are pleasant. In the centre, with its stone paving and crowds, walking is usually quicker.
