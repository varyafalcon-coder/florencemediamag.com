## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">Ski the Olympic downhill in the morning, sit in a Roman hot spring in the afternoon.</p>

A real medieval town with stone streets and towers, one of the longest runs in Italy from 3,000 metres to the houses, and the thermal baths at the end of the day. Good value for this altitude.

## A perfect day

<ol class="dayplan">
<li><span class="when">Morning</span><span class="what">Straight up to Cima Bianca while the snow is firm.</span></li>

## Before you go

<ul class="tips">
<li>40&nbsp;€ in early December, 59&nbsp;€, 65&nbsp;€ at peak dates. Buy online for less.</li>
<li>The Stelvio is a racing piste. Leave it to the afternoon only if you ski well.</li>
<li>Book the thermal baths at weekends.</li>
