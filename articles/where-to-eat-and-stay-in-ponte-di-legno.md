  - q: "How far is Ponte di Legno from Florence?"
    a: "About four and a half hours by car, via Brescia and Val Camonica or via Trento and Val di Sole. By train, go to Brescia, take the local line to Edolo and a bus up the valley."
  - q: "What is the best restaurant near Ponte di Legno?"
    a: "Kro, in Pontagna di Temù about ten minutes below the village, listed in the Michelin guide. Alpine and regional cooking in converted stone stables: mushrooms and handmade casoncelli. Check closing days before you go."
  - q: "What is the typical food in Ponte di Legno?"
    a: "Casoncelli, a filled pasta from the Brescia mountains, gnoc de la cua, the village's own soft gnocchi, Silter, the protected cheese of Val Camonica, and polenta with game or mushrooms."
  - q: "Is it better to stay in Ponte di Legno or at Passo del Tonale?"
    a: "Ponte di Legno for a real village with restaurants and evenings, Passo del Tonale if you want to step out of the hotel straight onto the slopes, with the gondola to the glacier right there. The two are joined by lifts, so you can ski both from either."
  - q: "How much is a ski pass at Pontedilegno-Tonale in 2026/27?"
    a: "A day costs from 52 euro, and 70 euro at peak dates: 26 December to 10 January and the whole of February. On most other days there is a 10 euro discount on the adult day pass."

## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">Ponte di Legno is the village, Passo del Tonale the ski station, and the Presena glacier above them reaches 3,000 metres. One lift pass covers all three.</p>

Ponte di Legno is a real mountain village, with stone houses, a church square and evenings that feel like a town. Above it, Passo del Tonale and the Presena glacier keep the snow white from November to May. It is Alpine skiing at Apennine prices, and almost nobody in Florence knows about it.

## What is on

Some events come back every winter. Dates change each year, so check them before you book. The village calendar is at <a href="https://www.pontedilegno.it/magazine/events/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">pontedilegno.it</a>.

<div class="events">
<div class="ev"><span class="ev-date">21 Nov – 6 Jan</span><p class="ev-name">Christmas market</p><p class="ev-text">New this season: about thirty wooden huts with crafts and local food in the village, at weekends until mid December and then every day.</p></div>
<div class="ev"><span class="ev-date">4–8 December</span><p class="ev-name">Snow Boom</p><p class="ev-text">A snowboard week with board tests, freeride and splitboard outings.</p></div>
<div class="ev"><span class="ev-date">6 December</span><p class="ev-name">Winter Opening Party</p><p class="ev-text">Music and a DJ set in piazza XXVII Settembre from 17:30.</p></div>
<div class="ev"><span class="ev-date">3 April 2027</span><p class="ev-name">Adamello Ski Raid</p><p class="ev-text">A ski mountaineering race for pairs from Passo del Tonale to Ponte di Legno, held every two years. Details at <a href="https://adamelloskiraid.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">adamelloskiraid.com</a>.</p></div>
