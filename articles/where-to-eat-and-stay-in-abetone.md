## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">This is where Florentines learned to ski, and it still feels like it.</p>

Wooden hotels, ski schools, very little pretence, and you can be skiing by ten after breakfast in Florence. Val di Luce usually holds the snow best. Weekdays are nearly empty.
