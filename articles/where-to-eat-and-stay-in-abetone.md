## What to eat up here

The Pistoia mountains cook like the rest of Tuscany, only heavier and with more chestnuts. Look for tortelli, handmade and filled with potato or ricotta, chestnut flour in pancakes called necci, porcini dried from the autumn, and wild boar. Mountain cheeses are pecorino and raveggiolo. None of it is light, and after a day on the snow that is the point.

The huts at the top of the lifts are fine for a sandwich and a coffee. For a proper lunch or dinner, drive down.
