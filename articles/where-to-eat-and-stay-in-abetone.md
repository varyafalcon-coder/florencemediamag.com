## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">Abetone sits on a pass at 1,388 metres, with the lifts starting in the village square, an hour and fifty minutes from Florence.</p>

A village on a pass with wooden hotels, ski schools and very little pretence. You can have breakfast in Florence and be on the snow by ten, and fifteen minutes down the road is one of the best trattorias in the Tuscan mountains.

## Before you go

<ul class="tips">
<li>Check the lift status the evening before. Abetone depends on the snow.</li>
