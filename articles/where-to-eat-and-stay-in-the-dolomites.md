## Which valley

<p class="lede">Choose the valley first, then the hut.</p>

<ul class="tips">
<li><strong>Val Gardena</strong> for a first time or arriving by train.</li>
<li><strong>Cortina</strong> for the scenery and the town. Expensive and proud of it.</li>
<li><strong>Madonna di Campiglio</strong> for polish: groomed runs, a smart village.</li>
</ul>

## Val Gardena

Train to Bolzano, bus 350 to Ortisei. Ortisei has the evenings, Selva the skiing.

## What to order

<dl class="dishes">
<div><dt>Canederli</dt><dd>Bread dumplings in broth or with butter.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Schlutzkrapfen</dt><dd>Half-moon pasta with spinach and ricotta.</dd></div>

## Before you go

<ul class="tips">
<li>Book the famous huts a day or two ahead in high season.</li>
<li>The Dolomiti Superski pass is 5% cheaper bought online two days ahead.</li>
<li>No car: train to Bolzano, Trento or Calalzo, then bus.</li>
</ul>
