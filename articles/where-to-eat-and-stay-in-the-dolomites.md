## How lunch works in the Dolomites

Lunch is the centre of a ski day here, not a break from it. The huts on the slopes, the rifugi and baite, are often better than the restaurants in the villages, and they serve proper cooking with a view: canederli, schlutzkrapfen, polenta with venison, kaiserschmarrn. Plan the morning so that you arrive at the one you want around noon, and book the famous ones.

Dinner is the other way round. Eat in the village or drive a few minutes out of it.

## Val Gardena

Ortisei is the village with the most life in the evening. Selva, higher up, is closer to the Sellaronda.
