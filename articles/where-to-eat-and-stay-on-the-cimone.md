## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">Cross into Emilia and two things change: the mountains get bigger and the food gets better.</p>

The largest ski area in the Apennines, with five base areas, so a full day never feels like the same three runs. The cheapest full day of skiing within reach of Florence, and tigelle in the huts.

## The mountain, top to bottom

<ol class="ladder">
<li><span class="alt">2,165 m</span><span class="lname">Monte Cimone</span><span class="ldesc">The highest peak of the northern Apennines, above the top lifts.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">Slopes</span><span class="lname">Passo del Lupo and Pian del Falco</span><span class="ldesc">The main bases above Sestola, with huts for lunch.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">Slopes</span><span class="lname">Lago della Ninfa, Cimoncino, Montecreto</span><span class="ldesc">The quieter sides of the area.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">1,020 m</span><span class="lname">Sestola</span><span class="ldesc">The main village, with most of the hotels.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">Below</span><span class="lname">Vesale and Fanano</span><span class="ldesc">Medieval hamlets and country restaurants.</span></li>
</ol>

<ol class="dayplan">
<li><span class="when">Morning</span><span class="what">Start at Passo del Lupo above Sestola.</span></li>
