## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">A mountain that works like a Swiss watch.</p>

One round summit with long runs down every side, almost no queues, and a train station at the lifts. At the top, two museums and one of the best chefs in Italy.

## The mountain, top to bottom

<ol class="ladder">
<li><span class="alt">2,275 m</span><span class="lname">The summit</span><span class="ldesc">Messner's museum, Lumen and AlpiNN, with the Dolomites all round.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">Slopes</span><span class="lname">The north side</span><span class="ldesc">Long runs down to Riscone and Brunico, about 1,300 m of vertical.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">Slopes</span><span class="lname">The south side</span><span class="ldesc">Sunny runs to San Vigilio and Piculin.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">835 m</span><span class="lname">Brunico</span><span class="ldesc">The market town of Val Pusteria, with the best dinner.</span></li>
</ol>

## A perfect day

<ol class="dayplan">
<li><span class="when">Morning</span><span class="what">Train or car to the lifts, and the long north side runs while they are empty.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Lunch</span><span class="what"><a href="/trips/alpinn/">AlpiNN</a> at the summit, then an hour in the Lumen museum.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Afternoon</span><span class="what">The sunny south side down to San Vigilio.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Evening</span><span class="what">Dinner at <a href="/trips/atelier-moessmer/">Atelier Moessmer</a> in Brunico, if you have booked.</span></li>
</ol>

## Getting there

<dl class="glance glance2">

## Before you go

<ul class="tips">
<li>About 80&nbsp;€ for Plan de Corones. Take Dolomiti Superski only if you will ski other valleys.</li>
<li>Book Atelier Moessmer weeks ahead.</li>
<li>The museums at the top have their own tickets and hours.</li>
</ul>
