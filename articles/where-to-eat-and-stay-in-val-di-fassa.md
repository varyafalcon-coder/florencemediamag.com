## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">The most skiing you can buy with a four hour drive.</p>

A long valley of villages from Moena to Canazei, the Sellaronda at the top end, and the run down the Marmolada glacier, the highest in the Dolomites. Busy and practical rather than pretty.

## A perfect day

<ol class="dayplan">
<li><span class="when">Morning</span><span class="what">Out of Canazei onto the Sellaronda, clockwise.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Lunch</span><span class="what">A hut on the loop. Keep it quick, the circle is long.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Next day</span><span class="what">Up to the Marmolada and the long run down the glacier.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Evening</span><span class="what">Dinner at <a href="/trips/malga-panna/">Malga Panna</a> above Moena, or by snowmobile to <a href="/trips/rifugio-fuciade/">Rifugio Fuciade</a>.</span></li>
</ol>

## Before you go

<ul class="tips">
<li>80&nbsp;€ on Dolomiti Superski, 89&nbsp;€ in high season.</li>
<li>The Marmolada lift closes in wind. Check it the evening before.</li>
<li>Book Malga Panna and Fuciade well ahead, especially at New Year.</li>
</ul>
