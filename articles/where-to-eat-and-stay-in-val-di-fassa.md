## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">Val di Fassa runs from Moena up to Canazei, with the Sellaronda at its head and the Marmolada glacier a lift ride away.</p>

A long valley of villages from Moena to Canazei, the Sellaronda at the top end, and the run down the Marmolada glacier, the highest in the Dolomites. Busy and practical rather than pretty.

## A perfect day

<ol class="dayplan">
<li><span class="when">Morning</span><span class="what">Out of Canazei onto the Sellaronda, clockwise.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Lunch</span><span class="what">A hut on the loop. Keep it quick, the circle is long.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Next day</span><span class="what">Up to the Marmolada and the long run down the glacier.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Evening</span><span class="what">Dinner at <a href="/trips/malga-panna/">Malga Panna</a> above Moena, or by snowmobile to <a href="/trips/rifugio-fuciade/">Rifugio Fuciade</a>.</span></li>
</ol>

## What is on

Some events come back every winter. Dates change each year, so check them before you book.

<div class="events">
<div class="ev"><span class="ev-date">Last Sunday of January</span><p class="ev-name">Marcialonga</p><p class="ev-text">The 70 km cross-country ski marathon from Moena to Cavalese, with races for children in the days before. In 2027 it is on 31 January. Details at <a href="https://www.marcialonga.it" target="_blank" rel="noopener">marcialonga.it</a>.</p></div>
<div class="ev"><span class="ev-date">Shrove Tuesday</span><p class="ev-name">Moena Carnival</p><p class="ev-text">A fancy-dress party in the square, with prizes for the best costumes and a DJ set to finish.</p></div>
