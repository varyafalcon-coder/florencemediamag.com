## First, which kind of trip

There are really two choices from Florence. The Apennines, Abetone and the Cimone, are close enough for a day out and cheap, but they are low mountains and the snow comes and goes. The Alps are four to five hours away, need a night at least, and in exchange give you terrain that goes on for days and snow you can count on.

If you have one free Saturday, go to Abetone. If you have a long weekend, go to the Dolomites.

The Cimone is the biggest ski area in the Apennines and a little higher than Abetone, which is why people from Bologna and Modena come here rather than to Tuscany. The weekday pass is the cheapest full day of skiing in this whole article.

The food is the other reason. This is tigelle and gnocco fritto country, and the mountain restaurants are as good as anything in the valley.

Val Gardena is the Dolomites trip that works without a car, which matters more than it sounds. The train to Bolzano and the bus up the valley take about the same time as the drive and nobody has to do the A22 in the dark.

Ortisei is the village with the most life in the evening. Selva, further up, is where you want to be if the plan is to ski the Sellaronda.
