## What to eat up here

This is the Aosta Valley, so the cooking is closer to France and Switzerland than to anywhere else in this series. Fontina is the cheese, and it goes into polenta concia, thick polenta with butter and fontina, and into fonduta. Carbonade is beef stewed in red wine. Finish with a grolla, the wooden cup of spiced coffee and grappa passed around the table.

The village restaurants are mostly ordinary. The good food is either on the slopes or just outside the village.
