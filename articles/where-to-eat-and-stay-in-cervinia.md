## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">The lifts reach 3,480 metres at Plateau Rosa on the Swiss border. That height is why Cervinia opens in October, and why wind is the thing to watch.</p>

Long, wide, gentle runs at altitude, and lunch in Switzerland if you like. The village is no beauty. You will be looking up at the Matterhorn all day.
