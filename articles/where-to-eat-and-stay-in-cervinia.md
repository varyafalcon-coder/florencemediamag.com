## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">When the Apennines are brown in December, Cervinia is white.</p>

Long, wide, gentle runs at altitude, and lunch in Switzerland if you like. The village is no beauty. You will be looking up at the Matterhorn all day.

## The mountain, top to bottom

<ol class="ladder">
<li><span class="alt">3,480 m</span><span class="lname">Plateau Rosa</span><span class="ldesc">The top on the Italian side, on the border with Switzerland.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">2,555 m</span><span class="lname">Plan Maison</span><span class="ldesc">The middle station, with huts and the terrace views.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">2,050 m</span><span class="lname">Breuil-Cervinia</span><span class="ldesc">The village at the foot of the Matterhorn.</span></li>
<li><span class="alt">1,520 m</span><span class="lname">Valtournenche</span><span class="ldesc">Lower down the valley, quieter and cheaper.</span></li>
</ol>

## A perfect day

<ol class="dayplan">
<li><span class="when">Morning</span><span class="what">Up to Plateau Rosa while the snow is firm.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Lunch</span><span class="what"><a href="/trips/chalet-etoile/">Chalet Etoile</a>, on the terrace facing the Matterhorn.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Afternoon</span><span class="what">Easy cruising runs as the light turns gold.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Evening</span><span class="what">Dinner at <a href="/trips/les-neiges-dantan/">Les Neiges d'Antan</a>, five minutes down the valley.</span></li>
</ol>
