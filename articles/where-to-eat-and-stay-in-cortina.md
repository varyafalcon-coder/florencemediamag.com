## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">Cortina is a town rather than a ski station: the ski areas surround it on every side, and the evening walk along Corso Italia is part of the day.</p>

Rock towers at Cinque Torri, the long run from Lagazuoi, the Olympic slopes of the Tofana, and in the evening the walk along Corso Italia. Cortina is expensive and proud of it. The huts here are among the best places to eat in the Alps.

## Before you go

<ul class="tips">
<li>Book Averau and the famous huts for lunch, especially at weekends.</li>
<li>The ski areas are not all linked: expect a bus or two.</li>
<li>Winter tyres or chains on board are required on mountain roads from 15 November to 15 April.</li>
