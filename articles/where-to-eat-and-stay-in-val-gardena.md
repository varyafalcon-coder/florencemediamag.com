## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">Three villages, Ortisei, Santa Cristina and Selva, line one valley under the Sassolungo, and the Sellaronda starts above Selva.</p>

Wooden Ladin villages under enormous rock walls, mountain huts with real cooking, and the Sellaronda, the ski loop around the Sella massif. Ortisei has the evenings, Selva has the skiing, and the train to Bolzano gets you most of the way.

## Before you go

<ul class="tips">
<li>Huts have walk-in tables on the terrace, but inside you often need a booking.</li>
<li>Buying the Dolomiti Superski pass online two days ahead takes 5% off.</li>
<li>Winter tyres or chains on board are required on mountain roads from 15 November to 15 April.</li>
</ul>
