## Why is it worth going?

<p class="lede">Courmayeur sits at the foot of Mont Blanc, 4,808 metres, and the Skyway cable car climbs to 3,466 metres from the edge of town.</p>

A car-free main street of old stone houses, the most famous lunch scene on Italian slopes, and outdoor hot pools facing Mont Blanc for the end of the day.

## A perfect day

<ol class="dayplan">
<li><span class="when">Morning</span><span class="what">Gondola up from town, the Val Veny side while the snow is firm.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Lunch</span><span class="what"><a href="/trips/maison-vieille/">Maison Vieille</a>, long tables and Mont Blanc behind you.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Afternoon</span><span class="what">Down by four, then the hot pools of Pré-Saint-Didier at dusk.</span></li>
<li><span class="when">Evening</span><span class="what">Dinner at <a href="/trips/pierre-alexis-1877/">Pierre Alexis 1877</a> in the centre.</span></li>
</ol>
