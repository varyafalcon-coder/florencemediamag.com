Florence Media is a guide to Florence written in English and checked on the ground.

Most guides to this city are lists of monuments and restaurants ordered by fame. They are hard to read and harder to use, because people do not arrive wanting a list. They arrive wanting somewhere quiet, somewhere with a view, somewhere to sit with a laptop, somewhere to eat for under ten euro. This site is organised the way the question gets asked.

Every place here was visited in person and carries the date it was last checked. Read [how that works](/how-we-verify/).
